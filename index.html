03 / SELECTED WORK

Projects that
move ideas forward.

A selection of projects developed during my ¡Supérate! learning journey.

YEAR 1
CV COMMUNITY · DIGITAL SOLUTIONS

CircleVibe

CircleVibe is a platform created to connect communities with solutions to their needs. It allows people to report infrastructure problems, such as potholes or trees that may fall, so the responsible authorities can take action. It also has a section where people can find nearby workers, such as plumbers and electricians, and see examples of their previous work.

My role & contributions: I led the project, worked on the design using Figma, and helped develop the platform with Power Apps.

View Figma design ↗
YEAR 2
TM ASSISTANT · ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

TeachMeNow

TeachMeNow is a platform that helps teachers plan their daily classes more easily. It includes a test to identify each student's learning style. Based on the results, an assistant helps teachers create lessons that consider the different learning styles of their students. The goal is to make teachers' work easier and create more interesting classes.

My role & contributions: I was the co-leader, worked on the app design, contributed to programming, and developed part of the AI code.

Visit website ↗ View Figma ↗
YEAR 3
VT REAL ESTATE · TRUST & VALUE

ValTrust

ValTrust is a platform where people can find homes for sale. It mainly helps Salvadorans living abroad who want to buy a house but cannot visit or see it in person. The platform evaluates different features of the house, such as the number of rooms, parking spaces, and other details, to provide a fair and reliable price.

My role & contributions: I put my organizational skills into practice and designed several pages of the platform using Figma.
